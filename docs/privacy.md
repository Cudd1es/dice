# Privacy Policy · 隐私政策

Effective 2026-10-10 · 生效日期：2026 年 10 月 10 日

[English](#english) · [简体中文](#简体中文)

## English

Dicide (掷定) is a dice roller for decisions and tabletop role-playing games, with an iMessage extension. It does not collect, store or share any personal data with the developer or anyone else.

**What the app does not do**
- It has no accounts and no sign-in.
- It has no analytics, advertising or tracking, and it does not use any third-party SDK.
- It does not connect to any server run by the developer. The app makes no network requests of its own.

**What stays on your iPhone**
- The last formula you rolled (dice, modifier, advantage, DC), so the panel opens where you left it.
- Your Settings (for example, whether critical success and failure are used). The app and its iMessage extension share them through an App Group on your iPhone.
- The app's Recent list (the last 10 rolls). It is kept in memory only and is cleared when the app closes.
- When you tap a sent roll in Messages, that roll is briefly passed to the Dicide screen that opens to show it, through the same App Group. It is removed as soon as that screen opens, or the next time Dicide opens.

None of this leaves your iPhone, except as described below. Deleting the app deletes it.

**Rolls you send in Messages**
When you roll in Messages, the roll (formula, dice, total and the optional purpose you type) is placed in an iMessage that you choose to send. Apple's Messages delivers it to the people in that conversation, as with any other message. The developer does not receive it. Apple's handling of iMessage is described in [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

**Crash reports**
If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your iPhone's settings, Apple may provide the developer with anonymous crash reports. This is controlled by Apple and by you, not by the app.

**Children**
The app collects no data from anyone, including children.

**Changes**
If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

**Contact**
Questions: open an issue at <https://github.com/Cudd1es/dice/issues>.

## 简体中文

「掷定」（Dicide）是用来做决定和跑团的骰子，带有 iMessage 扩展。它不会收集、保存或向开发者及任何人分享你的个人数据。

**App 不会做的事**
- 没有账号，不需要登录。
- 没有统计分析、广告或追踪，也没有使用任何第三方 SDK。
- 不连接开发者的任何服务器。App 本身不发出任何网络请求。

**保存在你 iPhone 上的内容**
- 你上一次投骰的公式（骰子、加值、优势、DC），下次打开时面板保持原样。
- 你的设置（例如是否判定大成功 / 大失败）。App 和它的 iMessage 扩展通过你 iPhone 上的 App Group 共用这些设置。
- App 里的"最近"记录（最近 10 次投骰）。只保存在内存里，关掉 App 后清空。
- 在「信息」里点一条已发送的投骰时，这次投骰会通过同一个 App Group 短暂交给随后打开的「掷定」结果页；结果页一打开就删除，最迟在下次打开「掷定」时删除。

除下面说明的情况外，这些内容都不会离开你的 iPhone。删除 App 时会一并删除。

**在「信息」里发出的投骰**
在「信息」里投骰时，这次投骰（公式、骰子、总值，以及你可选填写的目的）会放进一条由你决定是否发送的 iMessage 信息。和其他信息一样，由 Apple 的「信息」送达对话里的人；开发者收不到。Apple 如何处理 iMessage，见 [Apple 隐私政策](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/)。

**崩溃报告**
如果你在 iPhone 设置里选择与 App 开发者共享分析数据，Apple 可能会向开发者提供匿名的崩溃报告。这由 Apple 和你控制，与 App 本身无关。

**儿童**
App 不收集任何人的数据，包括儿童。

**变更**
本政策如有变更，新版本会发布在本页，并更新生效日期。

**联系方式**
如有问题，请在 <https://github.com/Cudd1es/dice/issues> 提交 issue。
