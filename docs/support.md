## English

**Guides:** [Quick start](quick-start.md) · [User guide](user-guide.md) · [Privacy policy](privacy.md)

**Common questions**
- **Where is Dicide in Messages?** In a conversation, tap **+** next to the text field, then scroll the list to **Dicide**.
- **My friend sees "Install Dicide to see the result".** Everyone who wants to see the result needs the app. Without it, Messages shows only the formula.
- **A roll says "please update".** It was sent from a newer version. Update the app from the App Store.
- **Why can't I see the result before sending?** On purpose: the result is fixed when you tap Roll and hidden until the message is sent, so deleting the draft and rolling again doesn't help.
- **Can I turn off critical success and failure?** Yes, in the app: the gear button at the top right, then Settings.
- **The purpose is cut off in the bubble.** Tap the bubble to open the full result.

**Contact**
Report a problem or suggest a feature: <https://github.com/Cudd1es/dice/issues>. Please include your iPhone model, iOS version and the app version (in the App Store or TestFlight).

## 简体中文

**说明：**[快速上手](quick-start.zh-CN.md) · [使用指南](user-guide.zh-CN.md) · [隐私政策](privacy.md#简体中文)

**常见问题**
- **「信息」里哪里找「掷定」？** 在对话里点输入框左边的 **+**，往下滑列表找到 **掷定**。
- **朋友看到"安装「掷定」查看结果"。** 想看结果的人都需要安装 App。没装的人只能看到公式。
- **投骰显示"请更新 App"。** 它来自更新的版本，请到 App Store 更新。
- **为什么发送前看不到结果？** 这是有意的：点「投掷」时结果就已确定，发送前对所有人隐藏，所以删掉草稿重投没有用。
- **能关掉大成功 / 大失败吗？** 可以：在 App 右上角点齿轮，进入设置。
- **气泡里的目的显示不全。** 点一下气泡，就能打开完整结果。

**联系方式**
反馈问题或建议：<https://github.com/Cudd1es/dice/issues>。请写明 iPhone 型号、iOS 版本和 App 版本（在 App Store 或 TestFlight 里可以看到）。
